Reading the colors (Location, Dynasty): plain text is as printed in the volume; italic amber text is derived — resolved some other way (map-matching, an index cross-reference) rather than printed at that exact spot. Hover either to see exactly where it came from.
Location vs. Place/Source: Place/Source is the find-spot exactly as the volume printed it — often structural ("ON THE SOUTH WALL OF THE SAME TEMPLE"), not geographic. Location is the actual place on a map that find-spot resolves to. Example: a record's Place/Source might read "SAME TALUK" (referring back to an earlier record's find-spot) while its Location resolves to "NARASAPUR TALUK, KISTNA DISTRICT" — Place/Source says what the volume printed, Location says where that actually is.
Citations: "Ibid."-style cross-references in Remarks have been resolved to their full citation — 99% of records that mention one (267 of 270, as of this build); the rest cite a different publication entirely (e.g. "A.R.Ep., 1914" or "Ind. Ant., Vol. XIX"), not another SII record, so there is nothing here for them to resolve to. The underlined word carries the full citation as a hover tooltip.
Filters narrow each other as you go — pick a Volume and every other filter's list shrinks to only the values that actually occur among that Volume's own records (a greyed-out option means nothing currently matches it; it reappears the moment you widen another filter back out). Reset clears every filter and the search box back to their starting state.
