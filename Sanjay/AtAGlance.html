At a glance

How these are counted

  • Source: the records published on this site's corpus pages, as they stand at build time. A record printed in two corpora (an ARE summary later published in full in SII or Epigraphia Carnatica) is counted in each. TN Inscriptions are the Tamil Nadu State Department of Archaeology's numbered and district volumes together with the Inscriptions of the Pudukkottai State.
  • Century: the first A.D. year in the Date field, or else in the first 300 characters of Remarks; Śaka years +78, Vikrama −57, Kollam +825, "Nth century" as its middle. Regnal years are not converted, so they count as Undated.
  • State: as shown on the corpus pages; Epigraphia Carnatica and Karnatak Inscriptions are Karnataka, TN Inscriptions Tamil Nadu. District: as printed (mostly colonial-era names, lightly merged: Tanjore → Thanjavur and similar); Epigraphia Carnatica by volume.
  • Dynasty: the printed dynasty grouped by keyword (all Chōḷa forms together, Vijayanagara's four houses together). SII often prints the king instead; well-known Chōḷa and Pāṇḍya kings are mapped to their dynasty.
  • Copper plate: ARE's Copper Plate section, or "copper plate" in the place or the start of Remarks. Hero-stone / Sati-stone: the hero-stone word-list classification shown in each corpus page's Type column (Sanjay pages for SII and ARE).
  • These are counts of what survives and was published, not of what was carved: a district looks dense where a survey was thorough.
