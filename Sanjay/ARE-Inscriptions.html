Reading the colors (Location): plain text is as printed — the page's own district/taluk heading. Italic amber text is derived — split out of the Place/Source text itself, where the page had no separate heading. Hover either to see exactly where it came from.
Location vs. Place/Source: Place/Source is the find-spot exactly as the report printed it. Location is the district/taluk it belongs to. Example: Place/Source might read "On a stone in front of the Anjaneya temple at Havinahalu-Virapura." while Location shows "Bellary district, Bellary taluk" — Place/Source says what the report printed, Location says the administrative area it's in.
