How to use Advanced Search

One search across every corpus on this page. Every control below narrows the results, and they all combine: a record must match all of them. Leave a control blank (or on “Any”) to ignore it.

1. Choose where to look

  • Corpora — click a corpus name to leave it out; click again to bring it back.
  • Volume / range — one volume (IV or 4), a range (4-10), or for the Annual Reports a year (1928-29) or range of years.

2. Narrow by category

  • Dynasty, District, State, Century, Language, Material (copper plate or stone and other), and where shown Type (hero-stone / sati-stone) — pick a value from the list. Choosing one runs the search straight away.
  • To pick several values at once, or to leave values out, use the ☰ button beside the list (next section).
  • The “Filtering on” line under the filters always says which filters the results are using.
  • These use the same groupings as the At a Glance charts: all spellings of a dynasty are grouped together, and the century comes from the record’s first convertible date. A record whose dynasty or date the source does not give will not appear under any value.

3. Several values, or everything except some: the ☰ button

Each category list has a ☰ button to its right. It opens the whole list as a checklist:

  • Tick as many values as you like. The box at the top narrows a long list as you type, and ignores capitals and diacritics (chal finds Chāḷukya).
  • Include the ticked values (the default) keeps records with any of them.
  • Exclude the ticked values keeps records with none of them. Records whose value the source doesn’t give are left out too, so “not Tamil Nadu” means a state known to be elsewhere.
  • Apply runs the search. The list then reads e.g. 3 values or Not Tamil Nadu, and the ☰ button stays highlighted. Choose Any in the list, or Untick all, to drop it.

Try these:

  • Inscriptions outside Tamil Nadu: State ☰ → tick Tamil Nadu → Exclude → Apply.
  • Every Chalukya line together: Dynasty ☰ → type chal → tick Chāḷukya of Bādāmi, Chāḷukya of Kalyāṇa, Eastern Chāḷukya and Chāḷukya (unspecified) → Apply. (Chaulukya (Gujarat) is a different dynasty; tick it too only if you want it.)
  • The Telugu country: State ☰ → tick Andhra Pradesh and Telangana → Apply.
  • Not in Tamil or Kannada: Language ☰ → tick Tamil and Kannada → Exclude → Apply.
  • Lists combine: Dynasty all Chalukyas plus State not Karnataka finds Chalukya records outside Karnataka.

4. Narrow by name

  • King — part of a king’s name, e.g. Rajaraja. Checked against the King field (and Dynasty, where a corpus records the king there).
  • Place — part of a village, taluk or district name, e.g. Hunsur. Checked against every place field the record has.
  • Both ignore capitals and diacritics: hoysala finds Hoysaḷa.

5. Search the text

  • Keyword searches every field of the record, including Remarks and its ID; press Enter or Search.
  • Match: Partial (default) finds words starting with your term; Embedded finds it anywhere, even inside a longer word; Full word finds only the exact word.

6. Read the results

  • The table keeps to what identifies a record. Click a row for every field; your search terms are highlighted there, and a tab holding a match is marked in yellow.
  • Results arrive corpus by corpus; the count at the top is final once loading reaches 100%.
  • Clear resets everything. The page’s address can carry filters, which is how a click on an At a Glance chart opens a ready-made search.

Examples

  • Hoysaḷa copper plates: Dynasty Hoysaḷa, Material Copper plate.
  • 12th-century records mentioning a temple near Belur: Century 12th, Place Belur, Keyword temple.
