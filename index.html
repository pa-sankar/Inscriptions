Inscriptions

A project by Sankar — Senior Project Consultant, Cystar, IIT Madras

Over the past several months, this project has built an easily keyword-searchable database from a major published source on South Indian epigraphy, with more sources on the way:

South Indian Inscriptions (SII)

The Archaeological Survey of India's long-running series publishing the full text of stone and copper-plate inscriptions across South India, volume by volume since 1890.

Annual Report on [South] Indian Epigraphy (ARE) Coming soon

The Archaeological Survey of India's yearly reports listing every inscription copied and examined that year — copper plates, stone inscriptions, and pre-1945-46 collections. Being added to the searchable site shortly after launch.

What started as a personal research effort has grown into something that may be useful to other researchers and enthusiasts working with these same sources — hence this page.
